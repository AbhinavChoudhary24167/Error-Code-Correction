# Limitations and valid claims

## Supported claims

- Registered code and implementation identities can be enumerated and verified within their declared logical universes.
- The matched campaign reports tool-derived physical observations under its exact SKY130HD/SRAM22, PVT, clock, seed, wrapper, and tool boundary.
- The v3.3 package qualifies 46 post-route activity-based ECC-logic records for the named SECDED/Hsiao operations.
- The framework can compute conditional models and Pareto/selection results when every declared operand and compatibility gate is satisfied.

## Unsupported or blocked claims

- No silicon or radiation measurement, measured Qcrit, or calibrated physical upset population is included.
- Logical SBU/DBU/MBU response does not establish physical SDC, DUE, SER, or FIT.
- Physical adjacency and interleaver protection are not qualified without a verified bitcell-to-logical map and event footprint distribution.
- The fresh 256×72 OpenRAM macro did not complete; inherited SRAM22 views are not fresh OpenRAM evidence.
- E5 ECC-logic energy is not complete SRAM, whole-memory, or system energy.
- No qualified SKY130 manufacturing inventory, yield allocation, packaging, replacement, or end-of-life population supports absolute lifecycle carbon.
- The five-seed physical sample is descriptive and is not a silicon/process population.
- No globally qualified ECC winner or universal scalar sustainability ordering is claimed.

## Generalization limits

Results are conditional on implementation, tool/version, PDK/library/macro, PVT, clock, workload, payload, wrapper, seed set, and functional unit. Transferring a numeric result across those boundaries requires a new compatibility argument and evidence record.

See [Evidence model](evidence-model.md) for qualification and [Roadmap](roadmap.md) for work needed to remove blockers.
