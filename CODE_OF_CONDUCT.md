# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and evidence-focused research community. Be constructive; critique ideas and artifacts rather than people; acknowledge uncertainty and prior work; respect privacy and licensing; and preserve negative or inconvenient results when they are scientifically relevant.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, plagiarism, fabrication or manipulation of evidence, and sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in repository discussions, reviews, issue trackers, and public representation of the project. Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior they reasonably judge harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer through the hosting platform. Do not disclose sensitive personal information in a public issue. Maintainers should protect reporter privacy as far as practical, consider conflicts of interest, and respond proportionately. There is no guaranteed response timetable.

Good-faith disagreement over methods, evidence qualification, or interpretation is welcome. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is not.
