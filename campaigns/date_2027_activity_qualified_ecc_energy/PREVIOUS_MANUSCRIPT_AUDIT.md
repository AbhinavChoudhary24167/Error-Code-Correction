# Previous Manuscript Audit

## Audited artifact

`paper/date2027_revision3/date2027_revision3.tex` and its compiled seven-page PDF were audited against the new DATE question. The previous paper's center of gravity is evidence identity across ECC semantics, temporal SECDED transformations, structural Hsiao variants, BCH timing behavior, and multi-frequency physical outcomes. That is broader than, and materially different from, the present activity-model question.

## Prior paper strengths retained

- Careful evidence qualification and explicit measurement boundaries.
- Matched five-seed physical comparison.
- Conservative interpretation of infeasible designs and missing cells.
- Reproducible open-flow provenance.

## Prior paper weaknesses corrected

- The earlier research question was too broad for one memorable DATE contribution.
- Multiple architecture axes competed for attention.
- Vectorless power could be read too readily as architecture energy.
- Operation definitions, activity coverage, warm-up, and exact operation normalization were not the central reporting contract.

## Non-duplication conclusion

The new manuscript is not a revision of the earlier story. It uses a subset of the same research infrastructure but asks a new falsifiable question: whether an architecture ordering survives a controlled change in activity abstraction on the same physically qualified implementations. The title, abstract, contribution list, main figures, results organization, discussion, and conclusion are new.
