# Reviewer Attacks and Prepared Responses

## Centrality and coherence

**Attack:** Why are temporal SECDED, structural Hsiao, BCH, and 5-ns evidence in an activity paper?  
**Response:** They establish which implementation dimensions must be controlled before activity-model substitution can be interpreted. They occupy one compact table and bounded discussion; Q3 receives all result figures and the abstract/conclusion emphasis.

**Attack:** Could the controls be deleted while leaving the main result understandable?  
**Response:** Yes, but the experimental justification for freezing route, temporal contract, target, and feasibility would be weaker. That is the intended balance.

## D9 attacks

**Attack:** “Activity matters” is known, and Ghosh et al. already used traces for Hamming/Hsiao checker power.  
**Response:** The paper credits that result. Its narrower contribution is a controlled ordering-invariance test: the same timing-feasible routes and SPEFs yield 5/5 Hsiao-lower vectorless signs but only 4/23 operation-specific Hsiao-lower cells.

**Attack:** Energy excludes the SRAM macro.  
**Response:** Correct. The abstract, figures, method, threats, and conclusion state the routed-ECC-logic boundary; no whole-memory or silicon-energy claim is made.

**Attack:** Synthetic operations are not applications.  
**Response:** They are controlled operation classes. The mixture equation is explicitly illustrative and no application-average claim is made.

**Attack:** Component decomposition proves a mechanism only after the fact.  
**Response:** It does not prove causality. The text says the reports are consistent with opposing internal/switching contributions and asks for delay-aware per-cell/net attribution as future work.

## D13 and methods attacks

**Attack:** Five seeds do not support statistical inference.  
**Response:** They are deterministic paired physical-design perturbations. The paper shows all pairs and uses descriptive sign counts/ranges, not population p-values.

**Attack:** The 5-ns data replicate the headline claim.  
**Response:** No. They are fresh routes of the temporal SECDED control and only establish condition-sensitive magnitude.

**Attack:** Conventional SECDED and Hsiao are not exactly equivalent.  
**Response:** Their internal codewords differ. Fairness is at the common interface and W1-correction/W2-detection contract; exact equivalence is reserved for the temporal SECDED and structural Hsiao control pairs.

**Attack:** “BCH failed” is unfair.  
**Response:** The manuscript states only that the named BCH(78,64,t=2) syndrome/Chien realization met the 10-ns target in 0/5 attempts. It is a categorical admission boundary, not a family judgment.

**Attack:** Hashes do not prove correctness.  
**Response:** Correct. Hashes establish identity joins; formal/functional qualification, timing admission, and activity coverage address different gates.

## Fatal attack threshold

Any route/SPEF/VCD identity mismatch, admitted negative setup WNS, incorrect denominator, macro-inclusive wording, transferred equivalence claim, or unverified central citation blocks release.
