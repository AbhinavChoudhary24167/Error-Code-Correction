# Figure-data readiness

Publication-quality numerical inputs exist for the parametric yield curve,
mask-NRE volume sensitivity, fab decarbonization intervals, and the normalized
manufacturing-carbon uncertainty ensemble. These must be labelled parametric or
normalized and must not be presented as measured production curves.

Absolute node, per-bit, lifecycle, GSE, interleaving, selected-ECC, and ranking
uncertainty figures are withheld. Producing an empty/status artifact is more
faithful than drawing those figures from unqualified point estimates. The CSV
records the admissible claim for each of the 15 requested figures.
