# imec methodological alignment

GREEN Matrix 3.0 is `IMEC_PUBLIC_EVIDENCE_ALIGNED`: it follows the public
methodological direction of a bottom-up virtual fab, process-flow and
process-step accounting, separate equipment electricity and direct gases,
explicit process-route complexity, yield/good-die normalization, declared
manufacturing assumptions, scenarios, uncertainty, and inventory-first impact
accounting.

This label does **not** mean imec certified, imec compliant, imec endorsed, an
imec standard, or a foundry-specific inventory.  imec.netzero is a model-based
virtual fab.  Its public descriptions and publications do not establish a
universal manufacturing standard and do not provide a matched absolute SKY130
inventory.

Public evidence is used primarily for model structure, causal direction,
process decomposition, trends, system-boundary discipline, and scenario
sensitivity.  Numeric anchors remain attached to their original node,
technology status (including projected nodes), model version, 300 mm/generic
HVM context, yield, grid, GWP/abatement assumptions, and exclusions.

The independent validation layer classifies each comparison as
`EXACT_REPRODUCTION`, `BOUNDARY_MATCHED_APPROXIMATION`, `TREND_REPRODUCTION`,
`QUALITATIVE_ONLY`, or `NOT_COMPARABLE`.  It reproduces linear Scope-2 and
abatement directions exactly as equations, advanced-route energy direction as
a trend, EUV route effects qualitatively, and rejects absolute SKY130 transfer.
