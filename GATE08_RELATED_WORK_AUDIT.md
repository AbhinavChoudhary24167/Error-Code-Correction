# DATE 2027 related-work audit

Audit date: 2026-08-25

Scope: ECC hardware implementation comparisons, SECDED/Hsiao/BCH microarchitecture, memory-reliability evaluation, cross-layer reliability/cost studies, post-layout methodology, and open evaluation infrastructure. Bibliographic identities were checked against publisher pages, DOI records, conference proceedings, author-hosted copies, or institutional publication records. Search emphasis was IEEE, ACM, DATE, TCAD, TVLSI, HPCA, ISCA, SC, and IBM Journal of Research and Development. This is a focused audit for positioning the present six-page manuscript, not a claim of exhaustive coverage of all ECC literature.

## Positioning outcome

The audit does **not** support a broad claim that prior ECC work ignores hardware cost or cross-layer trade-offs. Hsiao established hardware-conscious SECDED construction; Ghosh *et al.* optimized Hamming/Hsiao checker power while monitoring area and delay; Reviriego *et al.* constructed low-delay SEC/SECDED codes; Naseer and Draper compared stronger correction with SEC/Hsiao hardware; BCH studies optimize decoder microarchitecture; and DATE work has jointly interpreted ECC strength with device/array area, energy, and reliability for STT-MRAM.

The defensible distinction is narrower: the present work preserves implementation identity, admits only correctness-qualified RTL, evaluates two temporally aligned exact-equivalent SECDED microarchitectures and a stronger-correcting BCH implementation under one pinned post-route flow, and retains missing/infeasible measurements explicitly. No audited paper was found that combines all of those controls for the same evaluated set. This is an inference from the focused audit, not a universal absence claim.

## Search conclusions used in the manuscript

1. Do not claim that ECC hardware cost, cross-layer ECC evaluation, Hsiao implementation, BCH microarchitecture, or reliability/cost co-design is new.
2. Do not claim that no prior work compares implementations of the same coding task; BCH and checker-optimization work do so in other scopes.
3. Position the paper around the controlled conjunction of exact semantic equivalence, implementation identity, common post-route constraints, timing-feasibility qualification, equal-work energy, and explicit missingness.
4. Describe the BCH finding as one RTL point, particularly in light of recent direct/conventional BCH architecture comparisons.
5. Keep weight-3 counts outside field-reliability comparisons; FaultSim and system-level works use explicit fault models that this study intentionally does not provide.
