\section{Experimental Setup}

All implementations protect a 64-bit payload. SECDED and Hsiao use 72-bit codewords; BCH uses 78. The container-pinned ORFS/OpenROAD flow targets SKY130HD at TT, \VoltageV~V, \TemperatureC$^{\circ}$C. Common settings are a \ClockPeriodNs~ns clock, \IODelayPercent\% input/output delays, \OutputLoadPf~pF output load, \CoreUtilPercent\% core utilization, \PlaceDensity\ placement density, and \WorkerCount\ worker. RTL, SDC, libraries, LEF/RC collateral, Docker digest, ORFS/OpenROAD revisions, trace, transaction contract, and power procedure are frozen.

Before execution, the seed rule was fixed as the first \SeedCount\ primes at least 11: $\{\SeedList\}$. For seed $s$, the global-placement, global-routing, and general OpenROAD seed controls all equal $s$ for every architecture. All \ValidRoutes\ routes are fresh Revision-2 runs; no historical seed-11 directory is reused, no result is replaced, and the Hsiao RTL is frozen before its first route. The sweep is a controlled sensitivity test of placement/routing heuristics, not a random sample from an OpenROAD population.

For each route we preserve stage status, raw reports, run metadata, source/configuration hashes, and a SHA-256 artifact inventory. Extracted metrics include final standard-cell area and counts; setup/hold slack, TNS, violations, and Eq.~\eqref{eq:fslack}; global/detailed wirelength, vias, congestion, and DRC; and, where eligible, internal, switching, leakage, total power, and energy/op.

The matrix is interleaved by seed: combinational SECDED, pipelined SECDED, algorithmic Hsiao, and BCH are run at one seed before advancing to the next. Each route must complete the common flow and preserve its final report even when timing fails. All 20 runs are DRC-clean and hold-clean; setup feasibility alone gates target-clock energy. The protocol makes SECDED power mandatory, Hsiao power conditional on meeting 10~ns, and BCH power unnecessary because its role is physical-feasibility contrast.

Power annotation uses the final ODB, SDC, and SPEF with the same interface VCD replayed across seeds. Activity is propagated into implementation-specific internal nets before a full-precision 12-digit power report. The comparison therefore retains clock-tree, buffering, placement, and routing consequences of each RTL. It does not isolate a causal energy contribution from registers or XOR depth, and it does not extrapolate voltage/frequency scaling.
