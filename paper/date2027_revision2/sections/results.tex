\subsection{Exact-Equivalent SECDED Robustness}

The routed SECDED contrast is stable enough to remain architectural rather than seed-specific. Pipelining changes standard-cell area by \SecAreaEffectMean\% on average, with a \SecAreaEffectMin--\SecAreaEffectMax\% matched-seed envelope. Detailed wirelength changes by \SecWireEffectMean\% (\SecWireEffectMin--\SecWireEffectMax\%), and the slack-derived frequency estimate changes by \SecFreqEffectMean\% (\SecFreqEffectMin--\SecFreqEffectMax\%). The pipe is larger in \AreaOrderingCount/\SeedCount\ seeds, more routing-intensive in \WireOrderingCount/\SeedCount, and faster by Eq.~\eqref{eq:fslack} in \FreqOrderingCount/\SeedCount.

The equal-work energy change is \SecEnergyEffectMean\% on average (from \SecEnergyEffectMin\% to \SecEnergyEffectMax\%), with lower pipeline energy in \EnergyOrderingCount/\SeedCount\ seeds. This is a measured result for the evaluated microarchitecture at a sustained common workload, not a general claim that pipeline registers save energy. It coexists with a seed-independent request-latency increase from one to three cycles and II=1 for both designs.

Variability is metric-dependent. The matched area effect has sample standard deviation \SecAreaEffectStd\ percentage points and only a \SecAreaEffectMin--\SecAreaEffectMax\% envelope; the wire effect has \SecWireEffectStd\ points of spread. Energy is similarly narrow at \SecEnergyEffectStd\ points. By contrast, the frequency effect has \SecFreqEffectStd\ points of spread and spans \SecFreqEffectMin--\SecFreqEffectMax\%. Thus its direction is robust in this sweep, while its magnitude is more sensitive to the placement/routing trajectory than area or energy. This is why all paired values are retained rather than replacing seed 11 with one aggregate.

The raw mean points give the architectural scale. Combinational SECDED uses \CombAreaMean~$\mu$m$^2$, \CombCellsMean\ cells, and \CombWireMean~$\mu$m of detailed wire; pipelined SECDED uses \PipeAreaMean~$\mu$m$^2$, \PipeCellsMean\ cells, and \PipeWireMean~$\mu$m. Mean slack-derived frequency changes from \CombFreqMean\ to \PipeFreqMean~MHz, while energy changes from \CombEnergyMean\ to \PipeEnergyMean~pJ/op. Both have zero setup violations at every evaluated seed. These are means of five fresh routes, not historical seed-11 measurements reused as samples.

Exact per-seed enumeration finds the pair non-dominated in \SecNonDominatedSeedCount/\SeedCount\ seeds. At aggregate means the relation is \SecAggregateDominance; the range-sensitive result is also \SecRangeDominance. The physical trade therefore cannot be reduced to one “better SECDED” without a system preference over area, latency, frequency, and streaming energy.

Table~\ref{tab:paired-seeds} exposes the exact matched effects that underlie the envelope and summary markers. Cell-count change is especially stable because the five routes map almost the same logical structure, whereas detailed wire and slack retain a larger placement/routing component. The maximum frequency benefit does not occur at the seed with the maximum area increase, so the seed cannot be modeled as a single favorable-to-unfavorable scale shared by every metric. Area and energy changes also drift in opposite directions over parts of the sweep. These observations motivate pairing each metric independently instead of selecting one apparently representative route.

The decision conclusion is more stable than any single magnitude. Every row keeps the same signs for area, wire, frequency, and energy, yet every row is non-dominated once request latency and the admitted objectives are included. The aggregate mean and conservative range test reach the same relation. Thus the five-seed result strengthens a directional statement---the evaluated pipeline consistently exchanges area and latency for frequency and streaming energy---without upgrading it into a population law. The remaining uncertainty concerns the size and portability of those effects, not whether one of the two frozen SECDED identities wins all admitted objectives in this campaign.

\subsection{Same-Code Hsiao Recovery}

The algorithmic Hsiao identity routes in five fresh runs and meets 10~ns in \HsiaoFeasible\ seeds. Its mean final standard-cell area is \HsiaoAreaMean~$\mu$m$^2$ (\HsiaoAreaMin--\HsiaoAreaMax), mean slack-derived frequency is \HsiaoFreqMean~MHz (\HsiaoFreqMin--\HsiaoFreqMax), and mean detailed wirelength is \HsiaoWireMean~$\mu$m (\HsiaoWireMin--\HsiaoWireMax). Its feasible-point energy statistic is reported in Table~\ref{tab:results}; an unavailable value is retained as a dash rather than inferred.

Relative to combinational SECDED at aggregate means, algorithmic Hsiao changes area by \HsiaoVsCombArea\%, cell count by \HsiaoVsCombCells\%, detailed wirelength by \HsiaoVsCombWire\%, slack-derived frequency by \HsiaoVsCombFreq\%, and energy/op by \HsiaoVsCombEnergy\%. These differences do not isolate a “Hsiao code penalty”: the parity-check matrix, encoder network, syndrome logic, correction matching, and routed realization differ together. Instead, Hsiao adds a third measured implementation in the W1-correct/W2-detect guarantee tier.

The result also distinguishes policy failure from physical inferiority. The historical table RTL stopped before mapping because inferred storage exceeded the unchanged synthesis-memory limit; it never established an area or timing point. The algorithmic RTL avoids that representation boundary while keeping the same limit, common floorplan policy, and code behavior. Its measured outcome is prospective evidence for a new hardware identity, not a selectively tuned historical run.

This point does not retroactively fill the old table-based hardware row. The exact proof connects both RTLs to the same behavioral/code semantics, but the measured PPA belongs to the prospectively frozen comparison-logic decoder. That distinction is precisely the identity discipline the paper advocates.

\subsection{Stronger-Correcting BCH Feasibility}

The evaluated BCH syndrome/Chien realization meets the common 10~ns target in \BchFeasible\ seeds. Across the sweep it has mean area \BchAreaMean~$\mu$m$^2$ (\BchAreaMin--\BchAreaMax), slack-derived frequency \BchFreqMean~MHz (\BchFreqMin--\BchFreqMax), and detailed wirelength \BchWireMean~$\mu$m (\BchWireMin--\BchWireMax). Its stronger W2-correction guarantee remains valuable, but physical conclusions apply only to this RTL and common boundary. Recent BCH architectures demonstrate that other realizations can occupy different cost/latency regions~\cite{lagendijk2026}; the repository contained no second correction-qualified $(78,64,t=2)$ architecture eligible for this campaign.

The timing miss is consistent rather than marginal: signed setup slack averages \BchSlackMean~ns and ranges from \BchSlackMin\ to \BchSlackMax~ns, with \BchSetupViolationsMean\ setup violations at every seed. Relative to combinational SECDED means, BCH changes area by \BchVsCombArea\%, cell count by \BchVsCombCells\%, detailed wirelength by \BchVsCombWire\%, and slack-derived frequency by \BchVsCombFreq\%. All routes are DRC- and hold-clean, so this is a completed physical point that fails the common setup target, not a route crash. No 100~MHz energy is displayed because that clock is infeasible for all five runs.

\subsection{Cross-Tier Design-Space Reading}

Within the shared SECDED guarantee tier, combinational SECDED, pipelined SECDED, and algorithmic Hsiao offer different physical choices. The exact-equivalent SECDED pair is non-dominated in every seed because the combinational design retains area and latency advantages while the pipeline retains frequency and energy advantages. Hsiao supplies the same categorical guarantee through a different code and hardware identity; Table~\ref{tab:results} makes its measured area, timing, routing, and energy visible without claiming exact semantic equivalence to extended Hamming.

BCH occupies a stronger guarantee stratum. If a system requires correction of every two-coordinate error, none of the SECDED points can replace it merely because they meet timing. Conversely, under the evaluated 100~MHz feasibility requirement, this BCH realization is not an achievable candidate. Exact enumeration therefore reports conditional choices rather than assigning a numeric value to correction strength. This preserves both facts: the measured SECDED realizations are physically favorable under the stated objectives, and the measured BCH realization guarantees correction that they do not.
