\section{Conclusion}

ECC code labels do not uniquely determine hardware cost. By qualifying identity before PPA and applying a fresh five-seed common-policy sweep, we turn a single-seed observation into a matched sensitivity result: the exact-equivalent SECDED pair preserves distinct area/latency/frequency/energy trade-offs across the evaluated seeds. Exact proof also enables a same-code algorithmic Hsiao hardware identity to enter the common flow without relaxing synthesis policy, while the BCH result remains an explicitly scoped stronger-correction feasibility point. The appropriate design decision is therefore conditional on guarantee, target feasibility, and implementation identity—not a family label or a scalar score.
