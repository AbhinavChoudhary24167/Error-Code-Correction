\title{Implementation Identity Matters: Matched-Seed Post-Route Evidence for Memory ECC Hardware}
\author{\IEEEauthorblockN{Anonymous submission}}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
An error-correcting-code (ECC) label identifies coding semantics, not a unique hardware or physical realization. We present an identity-preserving evaluation method that qualifies correctness before physical measurement and applies one pinned RTL-to-route and equal-work power policy over \SeedCount\ predeclared matched seeds. For exact-equivalent combinational and pipelined SECDED (72,64), the pipeline changes area by \SecAreaEffectMean\% on average (range \SecAreaEffectMin--\SecAreaEffectMax\%), slack-derived frequency by \SecFreqEffectMean\% (\SecFreqEffectMin--\SecFreqEffectMax\%), and steady-stream energy/op by \SecEnergyEffectMean\% (from \SecEnergyEffectMin\% to \SecEnergyEffectMax\%). Area, frequency, and energy orderings occur in \AreaOrderingCount/\SeedCount, \FreqOrderingCount/\SeedCount, and \EnergyOrderingCount/\SeedCount\ matched seeds, respectively; request latency remains one versus three cycles. We also prove exact behavioral identity for an algorithmic Hsiao decoder before routing it under the same sweep; it meets the \ClockPeriodNs~ns target in \HsiaoFeasible\ runs. The evaluated shortened BCH (78,64,$t=2$) realization meets that target in \BchFeasible\ runs. These are controlled heuristic-sensitivity envelopes, not population estimates or family-wide rankings. The results show that correctness evidence, RTL identity, target feasibility, and physical variability must remain explicit when comparing ECC hardware.
\end{abstract}
