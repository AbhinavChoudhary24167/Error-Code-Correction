\section{Experimental Setup}

All designs protect a 64-bit useful payload. Conventional and Hsiao SECDED use 72-bit codewords; BCH uses 78 bits. The ORFS/OpenROAD toolchain is container-pinned. The platform is SKY130HD at the TT, 1.80 V, 25~$^{\circ}$C corner. The common target is 10 ns (100 MHz), with input and output delays each set to 10\% of the period, a 0.05 pF output load, 35\% core-utilization policy, and 0.55 placement density. Physical runs use deterministic seed 11 and one worker. Encoder and decoder channels process each accepted transaction in parallel.

The common constraint is a reference point, not a promise that every design will meet it. Each implementation receives the same flow stages and policy; no candidate-specific clock relaxation, floorplan tuning, utilization adjustment, or post-result retry is used. A prospective run is retained even when slack is negative. This matters for BCH: relaxing its clock after the 10 ns miss would answer a different question and would make its power result incomparable with the target-clock SECDED measurements.

Hsiao is correctness/reliability-qualified, but its inferred $256\times73$ table exceeded the unchanged 4096-bit ORFS synthesis-memory policy. The policy was not relaxed after observing the outcome, so Hsiao remains ``PPA unavailable'' and is excluded from physical conclusions. This status does not imply physical inferiority.

The reported experiment consists of one qualified physical policy and a limited set of implementations, not a seed distribution, process-variation study, multi-corner signoff, or silicon measurement. The artifact package records the pinned image/tool revisions, accepted identities, raw sources, figure transformations, and numerical checks needed to reproduce the manuscript tables and plots.

Post-route area is read from the final design database, timing from the common SDC analysis, and wirelength from detailed routing. Parasitics and activity are propagated for power characterization. The artifact preserves machine-readable implementation identifiers and evidence status beside every value, rather than relying on display labels. Qualification repeats under the fixed seed showed no observed metric variation, but we make no general determinism claim for other hosts, worker counts, or tool revisions.

The manuscript itself is generated from a frozen claim registry. Tables, plots, and numeric macros share the same authoritative evidence source; a semantic validator independently checks the headline quantities and prohibited interpretations. Thus, displayed rounding can be traced to full-precision values without hand-copying numbers between prose and figures.
