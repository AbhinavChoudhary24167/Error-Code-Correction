\section{Conclusion}

ECC selection should preserve implementation identity rather than rely on coding capability alone. Under one pinned post-route flow, exact-equivalent combinational and pipelined SECDED architectures exhibit distinct, non-dominated physical trade-offs, while the stronger-correcting evaluated BCH RTL incurs substantial routed cost and misses the common timing target. A reproducible comparison can expose these choices without treating unavailable or infeasible evidence as favorable measurements. Broader conclusions require more technologies, implementations, corners, and physically grounded fault models.
