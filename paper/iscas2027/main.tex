\title{Evidence-Qualified Reliability--Energy Decision Boundaries for SRAM Protection}

\author{\IEEEauthorblockN{Author Name(s) Required Before Submission}
\IEEEauthorblockA{Affiliation(s) and email address(es) required; this is not an anonymous submission}}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Selecting SRAM protection from area, timing, energy, and carbon estimates is
unsafe when the estimates do not describe the same implementation or evidence
boundary. We present a fail-closed decision method that admits an architecture
only after its protection service, implementation identity, routed setup and
hold feasibility, operation-resolved activity, and lifecycle scope are mutually
consistent. We apply it to an unprotected baseline, two SECDED organizations,
and a stronger BCH identity in a pinned open SKY130 flow. The primary
energy population reduces to \PrimarySeedCount{} fully timing-clean matched
SECDED pairs and \PrimaryEfiveRecordCount{} post-route ECC-logic records.
Hsiao is lower energy in only \HsiaoLowerCount{} of
\PrimaryComparisonCount{} paired operation cells; the sign depends on operation
mix. The routed BCH identity provides stronger declared correction but is
setup-clean in \BchCleanCount{}/\PhysicalSeedCount{} seeds at the common target.
We derive a workload boundary and a symbolic lifecycle break-even condition,
while leaving SRAM-macro energy, physical failure rates, and SKY130 embodied
carbon explicitly unresolved. Thus the result is a qualified decision region,
not a global ``greenest ECC'' ranking.
\end{abstract}

\begin{IEEEkeywords}
SRAM, error-correcting codes, post-route activity, energy, carbon accounting,

\section{Introduction}
Memory-ECC comparisons commonly place correction capability, implementation
cost, and energy on one Pareto plot. Yet a point may combine a logical guarantee
from one code, timing from another wrapper, vectorless power, and a manufacturing
coefficient from a different process boundary. The resulting precision is
numerical rather than evidential. This problem becomes acute for sustainability:
operational energy is workload-dependent, while embodied carbon depends on
process, yield, geography, and system boundary~\cite{gupta2022act,boakes2023,pirson2023,chen2025carbonclarity}.

We ask: \emph{which SRAM-protection preferences remain identifiable after
categorical service, exact implementation identity, full timing feasibility,
operation energy, and lifecycle assumptions are joined without imputing missing
quantities?} Our contributions are:
(i) a fail-closed evidence-admission procedure; (ii) a matched open-flow study
that retains infeasible and incomplete candidates as negative results; and
(iii) measured-coefficient workload and symbolic lifecycle decision boundaries.
We do not claim a new code, activity model, global winner, field failure rate,
or node-calibrated lifecycle result.

\section{Prior Work and Gap}
Hsiao established sparse odd-weight-column SECDED construction~\cite{hsiao1970};
Ghosh \emph{et al.} showed that parity-check structure and input ordering affect
memory-checker power under traces~\cite{ghosh2004}. Cache-ECC and low-delay-code
studies establish reliability--area--performance trade-offs and hardware-aware
construction~\cite{rossi2011,reviriego2013}. Adaptive protection is also prior
art~\cite{lee2022}, as is activity-aware power estimation~\cite{najm1993}.
OpenROAD and OpenRAM provide inspectable implementation infrastructure
~\cite{ajayi2019,guthaus2016}.

These works do not make it valid to merge unlike evidence. Physical upset
topology is a required link between cell events and logical code service
~\cite{clemente2021,wirthlin2014}; semiconductor LCA likewise requires aligned
scope and assumptions~\cite{garciabardon2020,boakes2023,pirson2023}. Our gap is
therefore methodological and empirical: preserve the evidence boundary while
determining which preferences are actually identifiable. A focused source map
and rejected novelty claims are archived with the artifact.

\section{Evidence-Qualified Method}
\subsection{Admission rule}
For architecture $a$, seed $s$, and operation $o$, define the admissibility
indicator
\begin{equation}
 A_{a,s,o}=G_a I_{a,s} T_{a,s} V_{a,s,o} B_{a,s,o},
 \label{eq:admit}
\end{equation}
where $G$ validates the declared protection service, $I$ fixes RTL, macro
organization, constraints, and hashes, $T$ requires routed setup and hold slack
to be nonnegative, $V$ requires workload activity and extracted parasitics, and
$B$ requires a common measurement boundary. Every factor is Boolean; an unknown
factor is zero rather than an invitation to substitute. Comparisons use only
matched cells with $A=1$ (Fig.~\ref{fig:pipeline}).

Table~\ref{tab:population} lists the implemented population. W1 and W2 denote

\subsection{Physical and activity experiment}
All retained physical records use the pinned ORFS/OpenROAD SKY130HD flow at the
TT, 25$^\circ$C, 1.80-V corner. Each architecture was routed at 5-ns and 10-ns
targets with seeds 11, 13, 17, 19, and 23. Final reports provide placement,
routing, parasitics, setup/hold timing, and route-DRC counts. Inherited SRAM22
256$\times$64 and 256$\times$8 LEF/GDS/Liberty views preserve macro identity;
their independent DRC/LVS and complete internal energy are not established.

For conventional and Hsiao SECDED, operation traces contain 16 warm-up cycles
followed by $N=\MeasuredOperations{}$ measured operations. Zero-delay activity
from the final routed netlist is joined to final SPEF. For the reported boundary,
\begin{equation}
 E_{a,s,o}=P_{a,s,o}\,T_{s,o}/N,
 \qquad \Delta E_o=\overline{E}_{H,o}-\overline{E}_{C,o}.
 \label{eq:energy}
\end{equation}
All \CoverageRootCount{} required SRAM-output roots are annotated, but
$E$ excludes SRAM-macro internal energy. The primary cohort is derived, not
hand-selected: both implementations must be setup- and hold-clean and all five
operation classes must exist.

\section{Results}
\subsection{Feasibility precedes energy}
All retained runs route to GDS with zero reported route-DRC errors. At 10 ns,
U0 and conventional SECDED are setup/hold-clean in every seed. Hsiao is
setup-clean in every seed but has a \HsiaoHoldMinimum{}-ns hold WNS in seed 11;
the primary matched set is therefore seeds \PrimarySeedList{}. The unpipelined
BCH identity is setup-clean in \BchCleanCount{}/\PhysicalSeedCount{} seeds with
mean WNS \BchSetupMean{} ns (Fig.~\ref{fig:measurements}a). Stronger categorical
service does not rescue an infeasible implementation.

\subsection{Operation mix changes the preference}
The primary cohort contains \PrimaryEfiveRecordCount{} E5 records and
\PrimaryComparisonCount{} paired cells. Hsiao is lower in

W2 detection have positive deltas (Table~\ref{tab:energy}). A single scalar
``ECC energy'' therefore conceals a decision variable.

Let workload fractions $p_I,p_R,p_W,p_C,p_D$ be nonnegative and sum to one.
The measured primary-cohort boundary is
\begin{align}
\Delta E(\mathbf p)={}&\IdleDelta p_I+\ReadDelta p_R+\WriteDelta p_W\nonumber\\
&+\CorrectionDelta p_C+\DetectionDelta p_D\quad\text{pJ/op}.
\label{eq:workload}
\end{align}
Hsiao is lower only when $\Delta E<0$. For clean operations
($p_C=p_D=0$), Fig.~\ref{fig:workload} shows the simplex partition derived from
Eq.~\eqref{eq:workload}; no workload frequency is assumed.

\subsection{What the carbon layer can and cannot say}
For positive grid carbon intensity $I_g$, operational-carbon ordering has the
same sign as Eq.~\eqref{eq:workload}. With an unknown embodied difference
$\Delta C_{\mathrm{emb}}$ and $N_{\mathrm{life}}$ lifetime operations,
\begin{equation}
 \Delta C_{\mathrm{life}}=\Delta C_{\mathrm{emb}}+
 N_{\mathrm{life}} I_g\Delta E(\mathbf p).
 \label{eq:carbon}
\end{equation}
If $\Delta E<0$ and $\Delta C_{\mathrm{emb}}>0$, the symbolic break-even is
$N^*=\Delta C_{\mathrm{emb}}/[-I_g\Delta E]$. The repository lacks a matched
SKY130 manufacturing inventory, so no numeric $\Delta C_{\mathrm{emb}}$ or
lifecycle winner is reported. Published advanced-node models inform the
equation and boundary audit only~\cite{gupta2022act,boakes2023,chen2025carbonclarity}.

\section{Discussion and Threats to Validity}
\subsection{Reading the decision surface}
The method changes the interpretation of negative evidence. U0 cannot serve a
W1-correction requirement; BCH is stronger but timing-infeasible as implemented;
seed 11 is not silently retained after Hsiao's hold miss; and incomplete macro
energy does not become whole-memory energy. The remaining comparison is narrow
but internally consistent.

This ordering of gates is intentional. A reliability requirement first removes
architectures whose declared service is insufficient. The physical gate then
tests whether that service is delivered by the named implementation at the
target constraint. Only equal-boundary, timing-clean implementations enter the
operation-energy comparison. Finally, carbon accounting distinguishes three
outcomes: an operational preference when $\Delta E(\mathbf p)$ has known sign,
a symbolic lifecycle crossover when embodied difference is unknown, and an
indeterminate result when measurement boundaries differ. Reporting these
outcomes separately prevents a low-cost unprotected design or a stronger but
unusable decoder from appearing on a misleading common front.

The clean-read region in Fig.~\ref{fig:workload} is not a recommendation to
select Hsiao unconditionally. It says exactly what additional evidence a design
decision needs: a credible operation mix, the intended clock constraint, and a
macro-energy characterization at the same activity boundary. Conversely, the
positive correction/detection coefficients matter even when faults are rare,
because scrub, test, or recovery policies can change their frequency. The
equation exposes that policy coupling without pretending to know it.

The BCH result should also be read as implementation-specific. The correctness
evidence admits its stronger categorical service, while physical evidence rejects
this unpipelined identity at the common target. Pipelining or iterative decoding
could change feasibility and energy, but also changes latency, control, and RTL
identity. It therefore requires a new transaction wrapper and a new matched
experiment; it cannot inherit the present record.

\subsection{Reproducibility contract}
The artifact separates immutable campaign evidence from paper derivations. A
single script hashes and reads the physical CSV, architecture audit, activity
matrix, campaign status, and run manifest; derives the timing-clean cohort;
recomputes all energy and physical deltas; and emits the figures, tables,
manuscript macros, and claim-level provenance CSV. The full campaign contains
\CampaignEfiveRecordCount{} activity-qualified records, but the paper's primary
numbers are generated only from the \PrimaryEfiveRecordCount{} complete records.
No RTL-to-GDS rerun is required to reproduce the paper because the paper reports
the frozen campaign rather than a newly sampled implementation population.
Exact commands, source hashes, units, formulas, and the distinction between
reproduction and expensive campaign regeneration accompany the manuscript.

\balance
\subsection{Threats to validity}

The experiment covers one PDK/library/corner, two clock targets, five
deterministic route seeds, one RTL identity per architecture, and synthetic
operation classes. Seeds describe implementation sensitivity, not a random
sample, so no confidence intervals or significance claims are made. Zero-delay
VCD omits delay-induced glitch activity. SRAM macro-internal energy, independently
validated macro signoff, Qcrit, physical bit mapping, radiation environment,
FIT/SDC/DUE, and lifecycle inventory remain open. A new macro characterization,
pipelined BCH wrapper, or deployed workload could move the boundary; each would
be a new evidence record, not a retroactive correction.

\section{Conclusion}
Evidence qualification turns an apparent four-way sustainability ranking into
a sequence of conditional decisions. The stronger BCH identity fails the common
timing gate, and the equal-service SECDED comparison reduces to
\PrimarySeedCount{} full-timing-clean pairs. Across their
\PrimaryComparisonCount{} operation cells, Hsiao is lower energy in only
\HsiaoLowerCount{}, with the workload boundary given explicitly rather than
collapsed into a winner. Absolute lifecycle ordering remains symbolic because
macro energy, physical reliability rates, and SKY130 embodied carbon are not
qualified. The practical result is not less information: it is a decision
surface whose valid and invalid regions are auditable.
