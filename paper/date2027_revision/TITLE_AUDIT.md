## Selection

Candidate 2 is selected. It preserves the current title's precise semantic-to-physical framing while naming energy explicitly for the primary D9 topic. It also avoids suggesting that every code has one exact-equivalent alternative or that cost differences are universally large. Candidate 1 remains scientifically accurate but makes energy, the primary track argument, less visible. Candidates 3, 5, and 7 are more memorable but risk sounding broader than the two exact-equivalence studies.

and design. The scores use those current definitions; D1, not D13, is the
